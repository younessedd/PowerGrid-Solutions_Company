Our History & Mission

Founded in 1998, PowerGrid Solutions began as a small electrical contractor specializing in industrial installations. Today, we're a leading provider of comprehensive electrical solutions for manufacturing plants, commercial facilities, and industrial complexes across the region.

Our mission is to deliver safe, reliable, and efficient electrical systems that power industry and innovation. We're committed to excellence in every project, ensuring our clients receive the highest quality workmanship and service.
